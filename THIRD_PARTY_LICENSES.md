# Third-party data and software

Third-party source files are not redistributed in this repository (`data/` is ignored). Everything below is downloaded at run time, with its version recorded in `out/manifest_*.json`, `data/cache/` and, for the sources of the four lines, with size and SHA-256 in `data/sources/record.json`. Derived tables under `out/` carry the licence terms of the sources they derive from (for ChEMBL, share-alike). Licences were checked on 2026-10-06 from the providers' own pages; the items not verified then were read on their official pages on 2026-10-18 (15 verified, 8 partly verified, 0 not verifiable; full records with URL, quote and missing parts in `out/license_check.json`). Where an entry says partly verified, the stated part is missing.

Method references (all verified against Europe PMC on 2026-10-06): Guney E et al., Nat Commun 2016, doi:10.1038/ncomms10331 (PMID 26831545); Cheng F et al., Nat Commun 2018, doi:10.1038/s41467-018-05116-5 (PMID 30002366). The authors' reference implementation has no licence, so the proximity method was reimplemented from the paper.

Reuse restrictions relevant to a public CC BY 4.0 repository (read 2026-10-18): CPIC is CC BY-SA 4.0 (share-alike, research purposes); GDSC allows only non-transferable internal research and educational use; HPO forbids altering its content; ClinicalTrials.gov requires attribution, processing date and a statement of modifications; OmniPath contains non-commercial and share-alike sources; Enrichr asks for a licence for commercial use; Europe PMC abstracts and full text keep their publishers' copyright. The repository does not redistribute raw source files, but derived tables under `out/` carry derived fields, so tables with CPIC-, GDSC- or OmniPath-derived fields should carry those terms or be limited to summaries.
