# Addendum - engine v4, LLM-read evidence at low literature volume

Written on 2026-10-15, **before any annotation input, LLM label, LLM feature, refit or score of this experiment exists**. Facts looked up before writing: the protocols and results of `addendum_engine_v4*.md` / `validation_v4*.json`, `protocol/annotation_guideline.md`, `out/engine/llm_agreement.json` (the earlier direction agreement: Opus vs Sonnet kappa 0.90 on 139 sentences) and (a first probe of the number of candidate pairs and sentences was started but produced no output and is not used); the counts quoted in section 11 come from the label-free `pairs` and `build` stages that ran after this file was written. This file extends, and does not modify, `addendum_engine_v4.md`, `addendum_engine_v4_dilution.md`, `addendum_engine_v4_ablation.md`, `engine_v4*.py`, `benchmark_v4.json`, `validation_v4*.json`, `engine_v4_frozen.json` or any earlier file.

## 0. Status and honesty clauses

- **Post hoc development experiment, not a pre-registered primary result.** The v4 decision stands unchanged.
- **Why.** The ablation (`addendum_engine_v4_ablation.md`) showed that at low literature volume the engine's advantage over popularity is carried by the PrimeKG prior (PRIOR-ONLY gap 0.059 on the sealed HELD-OUT at every fraction; LITERATURE-ONLY gap negative; the co-mention features add nothing at 1-3 % of the literature). The **hypothesis written beforehand** is: *evidence read by an LLM (direction and relevance labels of sentences that name the drug in a paper retrieved for the disease, each sentence verified verbatim against the source) contributes at low volume where co-mention counts do not.*
- **Failure is reported as is.** If the lower bound of the paired difference is not above 0 at both fractions, the hypothesis is reported as **not supported** (partial if only one fraction), and the feature is not adopted.
- **The HELD-OUT set has already been opened three times** (v4, dilution, ablation); this is a further look at a sealed-but-opened set, scored once more under a lock. No model selection uses it.
- Every number is produced by `src/track2/engine_v4_llm.py` (feature construction and scoring) from labels in `out/engine/llm_labels/`. Seed everywhere: **20261015**. Limitation stated now: random thinning of papers is a stress test of volume dependence, not a measurement on rare diseases (as in the dilution protocol); LLM annotators are not independent of each other (same family) and know the drugs.

## 1. The three LLM uses

1. **Direction labels** (`benefit / harm / no_effect / unclear` plus confidence 1-3) with the existing, unchanged `protocol/annotation_guideline.md`. Two blind annotators (the two models of `out/engine/annotations_opus.json` / `annotations_sonnet.json`: Opus and Sonnet) each see only the item (drug, disease name, sentence, paper id) and the guideline. The deterministic rule extractor `engine_extractors.rule_direction` is the third reader. The **reliability flag** of a sentence is the project's `engine_llm_agreement.reliability` applied to the three direction labels (Opus, Sonnet, rule): `robust` (all three agree), `majority` (two agree), `unresolved` (all differ). The flag is a property of the label, not a correction of it.
2. **Relevance labels** (`yes / no / unclear` plus confidence 1-3): does the sentence evaluate the drug **as a treatment of the disease, or of its mechanism class**? New guideline `protocol/relevance_guideline.md`, written for this experiment before any item exists. The annotator sees **the sentence, the drug and the disease name only**; outside knowledge is forbidden by the guideline. Same two blind annotators.
3. **Verbatim verification.** Every sentence is checked against the Europe PMC source text of its paper (title + abstract, as re-fetched from Europe PMC), with the project's existing rule of `track2.verify_literature` (`squash`: tags removed, HTML entities decoded, all whitespace ignored, nothing else normalised), with one narrow, declared exception: the engine's retrieval stores `title + ". " + abstract`, so a title sentence carries one period that the Europe PMC text does not have; if a sentence fails only for that reason (it is verbatim once its single last period is removed), the sentence is stored without that period (`repair_join_period`), and the result is then checked by the strict rule. Any other mismatch (for example markup that the engine's sentence splitter removed) leaves the sentence `not_found`. `engine_v4_llm.verify_sentences` imports `squash` unchanged; the only difference from `verify_literature.check` is that source texts are fetched in batches by external id (`EXT_ID` OR query, `pageSize` 100, `cursorMark`-free because ids are exact; at most 4 requests per second; each response cached on disk by `http_cache`), because the number of papers is in the thousands. A sentence that is `not_found` or whose paper is `not_checkable` is **dropped from the items before annotation** (annotation tokens are not spent on it) and counted in the manifest. The annotators' labels are never used to decide verification.

## 2. Pairs to annotate (budget control)

For every benchmark_v4 disease (103 DEV, 42 HELD-OUT), the candidate set **P_d** is the union of:
- the **30 drugs with the highest frozen B0 score** at f = 1.0 (B0 head of `engine_v4_frozen.json`, hashes verified as in the dilution protocol; label-free);
- every **gold-1** drug of the disease; every **gold-3** (openFDA label) drug; every **gold-2** (ClinicalTrials.gov) drug **where gold 2 exists**. `benchmark_v4.json` stores gold 1 and gold 3 only; gold 2 exists only in `benchmark_v2.json` (the 45 diseases inherited from v2). Gold 2 is used for those 45 and is absent for the other 100 diseases (**deviation from a uniform rule, stated, not repaired** because building gold 2 needs ClinicalTrials.gov queries that the task excludes).

For each pair (d, drug in P_d): the papers are the cached Europe PMC retrieval of the disease (`engine_score.fetch_papers`, cache only), kept in retrieval order; only papers that contain a disease gene symbol count (the v1 paper gate); sentences are `elc.split_sentences` sentences that match the v1 drug regex for that drug. **At most 3 papers per pair and at most 2 sentences per paper** (the first two in sentence order) are annotated, **counted after thinning**: for a given sample (fraction, replicate), the 3 papers are the first 3 papers, in retrieval order, among the papers kept by that sample that mention the drug.

**Annotate once, reuse everywhere.** Thinning at fraction f is a random subset of the same retrieved papers (`default_rng([20261015, rank, frac_index, rep])`, exactly the dilution/ablation subsamples). The first two drug sentences of a paper depend only on the paper and the drug. Therefore the items of a pair are the **union, over the 21 samples (f = 1.0 once; f = 0.3, 0.1, 0.03, 0.01 with 5 replicates each), of the sentences of its up-to-3 papers**; each distinct (disease, drug, paper, sentence) is annotated **once**, and every fraction and replicate reuses the label of the papers it keeps. No label is produced for a sample separately. Consequence, stated: the counts of the features are capped (at most 3 papers x 2 sentences = 6 sentences per pair, at every fraction), so at f = 1.0 they are truncated counts, and a pair outside P_d has no annotated sentence (see section 8, selection asymmetry).

## 3. Features (per disease, drug, sample)

From the verified sentences of the kept papers (up to 3 papers, 2 sentences each) with labels:
- a sentence is **relevant** if both blind annotators say `relevance = yes` (strict conjunction; any disagreement or `unclear` makes it not relevant);
- its **direction** is the label that at least two of (Opus, Sonnet, rule) share (`robust` or `majority`); `unresolved` sentences are excluded from all counts;
- `n_rel_benefit` = number of relevant sentences with direction `benefit`; `n_rel_harm` = number of relevant sentences with direction `harm`; `n_rel_robust` = number of the counted (relevant, direction `benefit` or `harm`) sentences whose reliability flag is `robust`.
Model inputs: `log1p(n_rel_benefit)`, `log1p(n_rel_harm)`, `log1p(n_rel_robust)` (names `llm_log1p_rel_benefit`, `llm_log1p_rel_harm`, `llm_log1p_rel_robust`). A pair with no annotated sentence has all three equal to 0. No gold, label, score or PrimeKG text enters the construction (checked by signature in the tests: no gold argument).

## 4. Models and refit

All logistic heads are the project's `engine_v4.fit_head` (L2, standardised features). Columns of the B0 feature matrix as in the ablation protocol: literature group {0,1,2,3,5}, prior group {4 (`log_popularity`), 6, 7 (PrimeKG)}.
- **PL (primary): prior group + the 3 LLM features** (6 columns). The question asked is whether LLM-read evidence adds to the prior.
- **PO (comparator): prior group only** (3 columns), refitted by the identical procedure.
- Secondary: **B0L** = the 8 B0 columns (thinned as in the dilution protocol) + the 3 LLM features; **B0** frozen (as the dilution run) and **PO-frozen** (frozen B0 with the literature columns at the DEV mean, as the ablation) as references.
- **Training** uses the f = 1.0 features of the training diseases (with the capped LLM counts), ATC level-3 class masking of training rows as in v1 (`engine_score._class_mask`), then the fitted head is applied to the thinned features of the other diseases at each fraction (as the ablation refit models).
- **Regularisation by nested grouped CV, DEV only.** Grid of C in {0.1, 1, 10} (UNVALIDATED, section 9). The **outer** loop is the v4 grouped 5-fold (`engine_v4.group_folds`; a group of identical gold sets is never split); inside each outer training set an **inner** grouped 3-fold chooses C by the highest mean macro AUC on the f = 1.0 features (same masking); the outer fold is scored with the C chosen inside. The out-of-fold DEV scores are the honest DEV estimate. For HELD-OUT, C is chosen by the same inner grouped 3-fold over all DEV diseases and the head is fitted on all DEV diseases, then frozen (coefficients, C, SHA-256) **before** the HELD-OUT labels are read.

## 5. Evaluation set and endpoints

- **Universe of evaluation = P_d** (section 2). Reason: outside P_d the LLM features are 0 by construction, and P_d contains all gold drugs but only the 30 best B0 negatives; scoring on the full 2705-drug universe would let "has an annotated sentence" encode gold membership (selection asymmetry, section 8). Inside P_d every pair had the same chance to be annotated. All models, PO and the popularity baseline are scored on the same P_d; an AUC on P_d is not comparable in level to the full-universe AUC of earlier protocols, only the **paired differences** are interpreted. A disease enters a metric if P_d has at least one positive and one negative (always true by construction; counts reported).
- Per disease and fraction the metric is the mean over the replicates (f = 1.0 single). Groups of identical gold sets form one observation (`engine_v4.agg_groups`).
- **Primary endpoint.** At f in {0.03, 0.01}, on the sealed HELD-OUT, the **paired macro AUC difference PL minus PO**, with a **95 % disease-level bootstrap**: 2000 resamples, percentile, seed 20261015, resampling the disease **groups** of v4 (a group is one observation; identical to a disease-level bootstrap when each group has one disease; the plain per-disease bootstrap is stored as a sensitivity), same indices for both models (`engine_v4_dilution.boot_idx`).
- Secondary (descriptive): PL minus PO at f = 1.0, 0.3, 0.1 (annotated fractions); B0L minus PO-frozen and minus B0; precision at 10 and recall at 20 within P_d; the DEV out-of-fold values of all of these; coverage (share of P_d pairs, and of positive and negative pairs separately, with at least one counted relevant sentence) per fraction and split; mean coefficients of PL; annotator agreement (section 6).

## 6. Quality gate: annotator agreement

- **Cohen's kappa** between Opus and Sonnet is computed on **DEV items**, on the relevance labels (primary gate) and on the direction labels (reported), with a bootstrap 95 % interval resampling papers (as `engine_extractors.agreement`; 2000 resamples, seed 20261015). The same on HELD-OUT items, reported after scoring and never used for any choice.
- **Gate:** if the DEV relevance kappa point estimate is **below 0.6**, the relevance labels are reported as **unreliable**, the LLM features are **not used**, no HELD-OUT scoring of PL is run, and the hypothesis is reported as **not testable with these labels** (not as supported or refuted). If it is 0.6 or more, the experiment proceeds. The direction kappa is reported, and its interval too; a direction kappa below 0.6 is reported as a caveat but is not a gate (the direction label also has the rule and the reliability flag).
- Also reported: label counts per annotator, share of `unresolved` direction, and the verified / dropped sentence counts of section 1.3.
- **LLM-knowledge audit (diagnostic, not a gate).** The guideline forbids outside knowledge, but an LLM knows what drugs treat which disease. Reported on DEV: share of `relevance = yes` among sentences of gold-1 versus non-gold-1 pairs, and the same for `unclear`. A large gap is read as possible parametric leakage of indication knowledge into the labels; it is reported, not corrected.

## 7. Decision rule (fixed now, HELD-OUT only)

**The hypothesis is supported only if the lower bound of the 95 % bootstrap interval of the paired AUC difference (PL minus PO) is above 0 at both f = 0.03 and f = 0.01** on the HELD-OUT. Only one fraction: **partial, not supported**. Neither: **not supported**. The gate of section 6 is evaluated first. The plain per-disease bootstrap is read as a sensitivity: a disagreement with the group bootstrap is reported. No threshold on the size of the difference is used. If the hypothesis is not supported, the model is not adopted and the report says so.

## 8. Leakage audit (L1-L6 as in `addendum_engine_v4.md`, plus those specific to this experiment)

- (S1) **Selection asymmetry** (above): gold drugs are always annotated, other drugs only if among the 30 best B0 scores. Mitigation: evaluation inside P_d only. Residual: P_d negatives are the 30 hardest B0 negatives (selected with full-literature co-mentions), so the candidate set favours drugs with literature even at f = 0.01; the same set is used for PL and PO, which limits the effect to the paired difference's level; it is a limitation, stated.
- (S2) **Gold of the HELD-OUT was read to build the HELD-OUT items** (P_d includes its gold). It is used only to decide which pairs are annotated, never in the item text, never given to annotators, and no shard item carries a gold or a score field (tested). Earlier protocols read HELD-OUT labels only after a lock; here the candidate list needs them, so the item build for HELD-OUT is a disclosed deviation from that habit. The HELD-OUT scoring itself still happens once, under a lock, after the frozen refit.
- (S3) **Items carry no gold flag and no model score**; item order is shuffled (seed 20261015) with opaque sequential ids, so neither id nor position encodes gold, drug popularity or score. Shards mix diseases.
- (S4) **Annotators' parametric knowledge** (section 6 audit).
- (S5) **Groups never straddle DEV and HELD-OUT** (inherited from the v4 split; asserted again in the code); the same paper may appear in both splits when genes are shared (counted and reported, not removed, as L5).
- (S6) **Features of training and test come from the same annotation run**: the labels do not depend on the split; the HELD-OUT labels never enter any fit.

## 9. UNVALIDATED values (each stored with `status = UNVALIDATED` and a `conservative_default`; none promoted by this run; a test fails if any lacks them)

Top-30 candidate size; at most 3 papers and 2 sentences per annotated pair; the conjunction rule for relevance; the choice of the 2-of-3 direction vote; the feature definitions of section 3; the C grid {0.1, 1, 10} and the inner 3-fold; the kappa gate 0.6; evaluation on P_d instead of the full universe; use of the f = 1.0 capped counts for training; the 4 requests per second limit for verification.

## 10. Single HELD-OUT use and lock

1. `engine_v4_llm build` (label-free): writes the shards and the manifest; reads the gold of DEV and HELD-OUT only to build P_d (S2). Does not read the labels of the annotators because none exist.
2. The dispatch of annotators, and the placing of their files in `out/engine/llm_labels/`, are done by the author, outside this module.
3. `engine_v4_llm dev`: verifies the annotation files against the shards (ids complete, labels in vocabulary), computes the DEV kappas, the gate, the nested CV and the DEV table; freezes the PL / PO heads (coefficients, C, SHA-256) and writes the DEV part of `out/engine/validation_v4_llm.json`. If the gate fails it stops after the agreement block.
4. `engine_v4_llm heldout`: verifies the frozen hashes and the split hashes, **creates `out/engine/heldout_v4_llm_scored.lock` before reading any HELD-OUT label**, scores PL, PO, B0L, B0, PO-frozen and popularity once, evaluates the decision rule of section 7, and refuses to run if the lock exists. Output with sorted keys.

## 11. Run log and deviations
(appended after the build and the later runs)

- **Build, label-free, no LLM called** (`engine_v4_llm pairs`, then `build`; seed 20261015; all numbers in `out/engine/llm_input/manifest.json`). Candidate pairs P_d: DEV 4442 pairs (103 diseases), HELD-OUT 1350 pairs (42 diseases). Gold 2 was available for 45 diseases (those inherited from v2). Pairs with at least one annotated sentence: DEV 2427, HELD-OUT 992. Annotation design: all 21 samples (all five fractions) served from one annotation, as in section 2.
- **Verification** against Europe PMC (98 batch requests at no more than 4 per second, 155 s): 4886 papers requested, 4886 with source text. Strict rule alone verified 6586 DEV and 2939 HELD-OUT items; 1508 further title sentences differed only by the retrieval join period and were repaired and then verified; after repair, `not_found` remained 67 (DEV) and 23 (HELD-OUT) and those items were dropped before annotation (mostly markup removed by the sentence splitter). Final items: **DEV 7637 in 77 shards, HELD-OUT 3396 in 34 shards, total 11033 items in 111 shards** (at most 100 items per shard). 155 papers appear in both splits (reported, not removed).
- **Token estimate** (1 token per 4 characters of the item JSON and of the two guidelines, 24 output tokens per item per task, one annotator, one pass over both tasks): about 13.7 thousand tokens per shard on average (maximum 14.5 thousand), about 1.5 million tokens per annotator for all shards.
- **Deviations and clarifications:** (i) gold 2 exists only for 45 of 145 diseases (section 2); (ii) the join-period repair above; (iii) bootstrap unit: the project's group resampling is the primary "disease-level" bootstrap (identical when every group has one disease), with the plain per-disease bootstrap as a sensitivity; (iv) the primary model PL uses the prior group plus the three LLM features and is compared with a prior-only model refitted by the same nested procedure, while the literal "B0 frozen features plus the LLM features" model is the secondary B0L; (v) evaluation on the candidate sets P_d (section 5) rather than on the whole 2705-drug universe; (vi) HELD-OUT gold was read to build P_d (section 8, S2); (vii) a first count probe produced no output (it was stopped) and was replaced by the `pairs` stage.
