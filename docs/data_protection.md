# Data protection

## Patient-derived data that exist

The proband's genome data (reads, VCF), the covariates derived from them, and a confidential clinical document were provided for the challenge. Per-variant covariate detail is generated by `python -m track2.genome_covariates` and exists only locally.

## Where they are kept

- Per-variant and other patient-level detail: `data/private/` (for example `data/private/genome_covariates.json`). `data/` is excluded by `.gitignore` and `.dockerignore`, so it is never versioned and never enters a container image.
- Raw reads and VCFs are not part of this repository and are not distributed.
- `python -m track2.snapshot` reads only `data/cache/` and `data/universe.json`; it never opens `data/private/`.

## What is published

Aggregates only: [`out/genome_covariates_summary.json`](../out/genome_covariates_summary.json) (sex, aneuploidy signal per chromosome, counts of variants in pharmacogenes and tumour-predisposition genes), HPO terms and general descriptions in the report, and the drug candidates. No genomic coordinates, sample identifiers, family information or per-variant lines are published.

## Rule on birth details

No birth-detail figures from the confidential clinical document are published anywhere in this repository, the report or the review page. Any new text or output must be checked against this rule before release.

## Re-identification

No attempt is made to re-identify the child or family, to link the data with any other dataset, or to contact them. Publish nothing that could narrow identity (rare-variant listings, exact dates, places, combinations of rare findings).

## Retention and deletion

**Deletion deadline: 23 November 2026.** The Official Rules (last updated 2026-10-13) require deleting all data from all environments, including intermediate and derived datasets, within 30 days of the close of the hackathon, and the organisers' announcement states that this means by 23 November 2026 (30 days after the submission deadline of 24 October 2026, 23:59 UTC). The deletion must be confirmed by e-mail to cnb@sagebase.org; prizes are released once that confirmation is received. An earlier version of this note (2026-10-08) gave 20 December 2026 as the author's date; that date is 27 days later than the rule allows and was withdrawn on 2026-10-18.

Scope of the deletion: `data/private/` (per-variant detail of the proband), every local copy of reads, VCFs and the clinical document (including backups and cloud copies), and every cache derived from them. The third-party downloads in `data/cache/` and `data/universe.json` are public data, but the author's instruction is to remove all data, so they are covered by the same command; they can be downloaded again from the sources recorded in `out/manifest_*.json`.

What is retained: source code, tests, the frozen protocol, the written report, the aggregate outputs in `out/` (which contain no per-variant or identifying content) and the candidate tables.

Mechanism: `python -m track2.delete_data` lists what would be deleted (dry run, the default); `python -m track2.delete_data --execute` deletes it and writes `out/deletion_attestation.json` (date, paths removed, byte counts, no content). Copies outside this repository (the Track 1 data folder, backups, cloud storage) must be deleted separately by their holder.

## Checks before any release

1. `data/` is not tracked (`git ls-files data` returns nothing).
2. Outputs in `out/` contain no per-variant or identifying content.
3. No figure from the confidential document appears in text, tables or the HTML review page.
